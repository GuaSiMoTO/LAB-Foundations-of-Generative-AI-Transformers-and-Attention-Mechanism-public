One rainy afternoon, Lily was walking home from school when she heard a faint meowing. She stopped and looked around, finally spotting a tiny, shivering kitten under a bush. Its fur was wet and muddy, and its big green eyes looked scared. 
Lily gently picked up the kitten and wrapped it in her scarf. "Don't worry, little one," she whispered. She hurried home, hoping her parents would let her keep it. 
When she arrived, her mom was surprised but smiled when she saw the kitten. "We can take care of it until we find its owner," she said. Lily named the kitten "Storm" because of the rainy day they met. For days, Lily asked neighbors if they had lost a kitten, but no one claimed Storm. She secretly hoped they could keep him forever. 
One evening, her dad said, "If no one comes forward, maybe Storm has already found his home... with us." Lily’s heart jumped with joy. Storm purred and curled up in her lap, as if he understood. 
From that day on, Storm became part of the family, always following Lily around and brightening her days.
